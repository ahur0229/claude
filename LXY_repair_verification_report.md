## 2. REPAIR TABLE

### DIII
- **LXY claim/step.** Theorem 4.1 / Corollary 4.16: for even r the sign-equivariant space S*(R_{H0}(p))^{K,χ} ≠ 0, proved by extending the orbital difference T° across the boundary orbit Kz via the published Chen–Sun Theorem 1.13/1.15 (Proposition 4.13).
- **Exact defect.** The extension step is false. On the semisimple slice at x0 the two orbit closures meet transversally in two complex axes {bc = 0} on which the stabilizer torus GL(P)/Sp(P) ≅ C× acts by (b,c) ↦ (tb, t⁻¹c) and τ swaps them. For the test F = φ(b)φ(c) with φ radial, equal to 1 near 0, the radial generator D = E_b − E_c gives T°(DF) = −2 although DF vanishes near the origin (Lemma B.1; I re-derived the integral). So T° has no invariant extension, even as an ordinary distribution. The published Chen–Sun Theorem 1.15 relied on Proposition 7.11 (all-degree vanishing for nontrivial finite-dimensional F), which is false; the corrected v2 (11 Sep 2026) Theorem 1.15 adds Levi/unipotent Schwartz-homology non-occurrence hypotheses in degree i (and i+1 for an isomorphism). In the DIII model H^S_1(GL_2(C);C) is the sign line under τ, and F^∨ = sign occurs in Sym⁰(N*)⊗χ, so the corrected hypotheses fail. The issue therefore persists after comparison with the corrected criterion.
- **Manuscript replacement.** Theorem 1.3(i): S*(P_D)^{GL(V)⋊⟨τ_D⟩, χ_D} = 0 for every r ≥ 1, via (a) I_μ = I_comm (verified: μ_a = tr(aM), M = B1C2 − B2C1, [Z_x,Z_y] = diag(M, −Mᵗ)); (b) GL tensor FFT ⇒ invariants generated by upper block traces of even words (Lemma 3.3); (c) the supertrace identity (Lemma 3.1, verified) ⇒ every generator satisfies t − τt ∈ I, hence every τ-odd invariant lies in I (Corollary 3.2); (d) Gaussian lifting Theorem 2.9 (verified line by line, including the operator identity dU(ia)(P0γ) = −i(F_a P0 + μ_a P0)γ, the equivariant right inverse, the local nilpotence of N = Fσ, and Hermite density).
- **Independent?** Yes: no use of Chen–Sun, of LXY §4.3–4.5, or of complex homogeneity.
- **Sufficient for global assembly?** Yes. Lemma 3.5 (re-derived) shows that for Spin_{2r} the multiplier group is the image of GL_r ∪ GL_r·τ, A = Z/2 for even r and trivial for odd r, so χ_D is the only character to test, and the consequent of (2.x componentwise) is proved outright. Ranks: all r ≥ 1 (so r = 4, D4 with triality, is included).
- **Verdict: REPAIRED.**

### Balanced CII
- **LXY claim/step.** Proposition 5.12 quotes Bulois Prop. 3.8: "all odd-length rows begin with the same letter"; Corollary 5.13 deduces that every p-distinguished balanced-CII orbit is block-swap stable with a τ-fixed representative; Proposition 5.14 and the stable density lemma then only treat even-block diagrams.
- **Exact defect.** Bulois's proof (cases (e),(f) of the table on pp. 54–55) only gives a_d b_d = 0 for each odd d separately; the printed global "same letter" conclusion is a quantifier jump. Counterexample (Sp12/(Sp6×Sp6), diagram 5_+^2 1_-^2): I rebuilt E = (L5⊗M) ⊕ M' with the stated forms and checked: dim U = dim W = 6, e ∈ p, normal triple relations hold, dim p^e = 6 with ad d-eigenvalues {6,4,4,4,4,2} (all positive, so p^{e,d,f} = 0: p-distinguished), rank(e²|U) = 4, rank(e²|W) = 2 (not swap-stable), T(e) = 36 = dim p (zero defect, so LXY's trace inequality does not dispose of it). Corollary 5.13 is false as stated and LXY §5's reduction to even-block normal forms does not cover this orbit.
- **Manuscript replacement.** Theorem 1.3(ii): S*(P_C)^{Sp×Sp⋊⟨τ_C⟩,χ_C} = 0 for every r ≥ 1, via symplectic FFT generators tr(X_{i1}X*_{i2}⋯), the same supertrace identity, I_C = I_comm (verified: μ_{a,b} = −½(tr(aL)+tr(bR)), nondegeneracy of the trace pairing on star-skew matrices), and Theorem 2.9.
- **Independent?** Yes. No Bulois, no distinguished-orbit classification, no complex homogeneity.
- **Sufficient?** Yes: A = Z/2 for all r ≥ 1 (center ±1; θ(g) = −g forces the block swap), the single nontrivial character is χ_C and its full-space vanishing is proved. Small ranks: r = 1 is also the Spin (4,1) block; both treatments agree.
- **Verdict: REPAIRED.**

### BDI / Spin
- **LXY claim/step.** Proposition 6.26 (even smaller block): a det_W-equivariant distribution on the regular set is produced by Chen–Sun extension across the finite Gram boundary; odd smaller block handled by a global vanishing. Corollary 6.27 concludes componentwise regularity.
- **Exact defect.** Same two-axis obstruction: at a scalar 2×2 self-adjoint Gram block λI+[[a,b],[c,a]], fixing eigenvalues gives a = 0, bc = 0; the SO_2 torus has weights ±2 on (b,c) and a reflection swaps the axes, so the Euler argument (value −4) defeats any extension; the corrected Chen–Sun hypotheses fail for the same homological reason. Lemma 6.24 excludes det_W only from finite-dimensional symmetric powers, not from the homology of the connected stabilizer.
- **Manuscript replacement.** Theorem 1.3(iii): both S*(Hom(W,U))^{O(U)×O(W), det_U⊠1} and ^{1⊠det_W} vanish in every positive dimension. Verified: Lemma 4.1 (det-semi-invariants of O_n on symmetric matrices lie in the commutator ideal; path-reversal sign argument is correct and exact), the Gram identities (4.3)/(4.4) (all re-derived), the determinant averaging over O_s(R), and Theorem 2.9 with L_0 = SO×SO and F = reflections.

The index-two decomposition (4.5) is correct (eigenspaces of a reflection). Both summands vanish ⇒ S*(P)^{K0,χ0} = 0 (Corollary 4.5).
- **Parity coverage.** Unequal odd–odd: pleasant (Lemma 4.3; correct, and it also covers r+s ≡ 0 mod 4 which Rubio's Prop. 4.18 wrongly excluded); |r−s| ≤ 2 (including r = s): nice, Corollary 6.2 proves the implication for every character of J/L (so a possible extra block-exchange component at r = s is harmless); |r−s| > 2, rs even: Theorem 1.3(iii). Rank-one blocks (s = 1) are included. No parity or central-form gap remains.
- **Verdict: REPAIRED.**
